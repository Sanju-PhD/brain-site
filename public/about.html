About Me

I am an AI researcher currently wandering through the beautiful chaos of medical image segmentation. I previously spent an unhealthy amount of time working on histopathological image segmentation and classification. Basically, I stared at microscopic cell images long enough for them to start staring back.

Now I have leveled up to the legendary "I have no idea what is happening anymore" stage with the HCP 2015 dataset. I managed to read the images, visualize them using a few tools, and convince myself that understanding data before throwing models at it is still a revolutionary concept in AI research.

Because honestly, blindly training networks without understanding the dataset first feels illegal. We must get our hands dirty. Preferably covered in preprocessing scripts, registration errors, and terminal outputs that pretend to be useful.

My greatest struggle? Having a roadmap and actually sticking to it instead of discovering 17 new research papers at 2 AM and suddenly changing direction like a badly regularized optimizer.
