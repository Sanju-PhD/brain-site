Spatial Pre-processing

Spatial processing: It has major two types: 1. Intensity transformations 2. Spatial filtering

What is spatial domain pre-processing? and how is it differ from transform domain pre-processing?

The processing occurs on a spatial domain means area or a plane containing pixels.

What is Intensity transformation?

g(x,y) = T[f(x,y)] where T is the transformation function, an operator being applied on the pixels of the image, f is the input image, and g is the output image. Intensity here is nothing but the gray level or a mapping function. S=T(r)

When we define a point from where contrast going from dark to bright or bright to dark, that point is called as a threshold point. before=r after=s 8 bit image has 256 entries.
